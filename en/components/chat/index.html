Chat

Chat is provided by lobbysvr's service/logic/chat/ and DTMQ; there is no separate chatsvr. World, private, and team channels share CS snapshot, heartbeat, and incremental-sync protocols. The team channel also carries team system notifications.

Channels and Persistence​

dtmq_channel_type in resource/ExcelTables/DtMq.xlsx defines channel types and storage policies:

Channeltypememory_onlyCurrent use
World1NOWorld chat; currently only partition 0 is used
Private2NOPlayer private messages
System notification3NOShort-lived system messages
System announcement4NOLonger-lived system messages
Team chat12YESTeam text and team system notifications
Orbit events101YESDOrbitRoomEventLog room events
Orbit chat102YESBattlefield/faction chat of an Orbit room; subscribed per config at room creation

DTMQ persists records for channels with memory_only=NO and skips DB saves for YES. World, private, and team messages ultimately reach DTMQ through user_chat_manager::send_event_message. A nonzero client-provided world_partition_channel is rejected.

CS Synchronization​

  1. chat_get_all_channel returns only channel metadata.
  2. Call chat_get_channel_snapshot for each target channel; active push starts only after its initial snapshot.
  3. chat_channel_heartbeat carries channel sync points. Lobby uses sequence/hash values to select incremental messages or snapshot repair and sends SCChatChannelSync.
  4. chat_send_message selects a world/private/team key through DChatChannelKey and submits DChatMessageContent.

lobbysvr_chat_cfg.push_message_per_second and lobbysvr_chat_cfg.push_message_buffer_max_count control push rate and buffering respectively.

Team Channel​

team_common_api.cpp builds the team chat channel key from (zone_id, team_id). The channel is created with the Lobby team cache and removed when the team becomes invalid or its last member leaves.

The channel carries two kinds of business messages:

  • Ordinary chat: atframework.chat.DChatUserMessage.
  • Team notifications: DTeamRoomNotification, including captain changes, readiness, matchmaking start/results, and battle-ready events.

Lobby maps DTMQ team events to SCUserTeamRoomChgSync. Clients must not handle text while ignoring team notifications.

Text Compliance Policy​

Text sending calls the compliance service first. Current chat policy is:

  • Compliance RPC failure or no result: log a warning and send the original text.
  • A match with empty filtered text, or only replacement characters remaining: reject with EN_ERR_TEXT_HAS_DIRTY_WORD.
  • Other matches: send the replaced text.

This fail-open choice belongs to chat; it is not a universal policy for all compliance callers.

Orbit and Battle Channel Boundaries​

When an Orbit room is created (user_orbit_manager::create_room), lobbysvr subscribes to EN_ORBIT_CHANNEL_TYPE_CHAT=102 chat channels in addition to the event channel EN_ORBIT_CHANNEL_TYPE_ROOM=101: lobbysvr_cfg.orbit.allow_battlefield_chat_channel controls the battlefield channel <room client_id>:battlefield, and lobbysvr_cfg.orbit.allow_faction_chat_channel controls the faction channel <room client_id>:faction:<faction_id>.

The subscribed chat channels are registered into user_chat_manager and reuse the unified CS snapshot, heartbeat, and incremental sync; channel metadata is delivered with DOrbitRoomUserData.chat_channel.

The send side is not integrated yet: CS DChatChannelKey still has only world/private/team keys, and user_chat_manager::build_dtmq_channel_key_from_chat_channel_key returns EN_ERR_DTMQ_INVALID_CHANNEL for other keys, so clients cannot send to Orbit channels through the chat RPC yet.

Implementation entry points are src/lobbysvr/service/logic/chat/, src/teamsvr/sdk/common/rpc/team/, src/orbitsvr/service/logic/room/, and DTMQ.
